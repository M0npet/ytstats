ytstats — Privacy Policy

ytstats is a personal, locally run tool. It requests read-only access (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly) to YouTube channels owned by its author.

Data is fetched directly from Google APIs and stored only on the author's own computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party. No revenue data is accessed.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
